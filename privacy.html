Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

lrod-analytics is a personal tool used only by its owner to read analytics for the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok accounts. It has no other users.

What it accesses

It accesses my own accounts through each platform's official API. It reads:

  • my own videos' and posts' public data, such as titles, captions, publish dates, and durations;
  • aggregate metrics, such as views, likes, comments, shares, and watch time;
  • the comments people leave on my YouTube videos and Instagram posts, including each commenter's username;
  • public statistics and public videos of a few other YouTube channels, for comparison.

It does not access private messages or anyone else's account.

Permissions

YouTube and TikTok access is read-only. Instagram's comments permission technically also allows replying to, hiding, and deleting comments. lrod-analytics never does any of these: it only reads, and I write every reply myself.

How the data is stored

The data is stored privately on my own computer. Access credentials are kept in files readable only by my user account. Commenters' usernames are stored only so I can find and reply to their comments. They are never published, and they are deleted 90 days after I answer or dismiss the comment. Backup copies are deleted within a further 14 days.

Processing by AI services

To summarize comments and draft replies for me, comment text is sent to Anthropic's Claude. Usernames are never sent, and @mentions are removed first. Voice notes I record for myself are transcribed by Google's Gemini. Gemini also analyzes my own public videos and the other channels' public videos. Each provider's own terms govern how it handles what it receives.

Sharing

The data is never sold, rented, or used for advertising. Summaries, including comment text without usernames, are sent to my private Discord server. Apart from that and the processing above, the data is not transferred to any third party, and nothing is published.

Google API services

lrod-analytics's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Data is kept until I delete it. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings, after which the tool can no longer read anything.

Cookies and tracking

These pages set no cookies and use no analytics, tracking, or third-party scripts.
